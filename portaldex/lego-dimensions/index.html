LEGO Dimensions collection tracker

PortalDex tracks every LEGO Dimensions character, vehicle and gadget and tells you which pack you need for the ones you are missing.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

What PortalDex covers

  • 352 characters, vehicles and gadgets from every LEGO Dimensions world.
  • Each item shows its pack (Starter, Level, Team, Fun or Story Pack) and number, wave, release date and abilities.
  • Vehicles show all three rebuilds.
  • "Which pack do I need" guide.
  • Android: identify LEGO Dimensions characters and vehicles with NFC.

Questions

Is there an app to track my LEGO Dimensions collection?

Yes. PortalDex lists 352 LEGO Dimensions characters, vehicles and gadgets with their packs and abilities, and shows which pack you need.
