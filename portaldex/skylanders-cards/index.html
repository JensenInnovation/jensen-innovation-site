Skylanders cards checklist

PortalDex includes a full checklist of Skylanders cards, so you can track your Topps trading cards, Battlecast cards and the stat cards that came with your figures.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

What PortalDex covers

  • 1,238 cards in 8 sets.
  • Topps Skylanders Giants (2012, 212 cards), Topps Skylanders SWAP Force (2013, 231), Topps Skylanders Trap Team (2014, 202).
  • Skylanders Battlecast (2016, 306 cards) with element and rarity (Common, Rare, Epic, Ultimate); silver and gold foils noted.
  • Figure cards packed with the toys: Spyro's Adventure (49), Giants (65), SWAP Force (84) and Trap Team (89). Completion counts these 287 figure cards.
  • Cards are sorted in checklist order by printed number, and character cards link to their Skylanders figure (and back).

Questions

Is there a checklist app for Skylanders trading cards?

Yes. PortalDex lists all 1,238 Skylanders cards — Topps Giants, SWAP Force and Trap Team, Skylanders Battlecast and the figure cards — and tracks which ones you own per set.

Does PortalDex include Skylanders Battlecast cards?

Yes, all 306 Battlecast cards with their element and rarity.
