What PortalDex covers

  • All six games: Spyro's Adventure, Giants, SWAP Force, Trap Team, SuperChargers and Imaginators.
  • 588 catalog entries: characters plus collector variants (Legendary, Eon's Elite, LightCore, Dark, Series 2 and 3 reposes, chase and event editions), 60 traps, 36 vehicles, 29 creation crystals, magic items, adventure packs and trophies.
  • Each figure's About page shows its element, game, catchphrase, attacks, Soul Gem and Wow Pow abilities, upgrade paths and tips for telling variants apart.
  • SWAP Force swappers are tracked as one figure; Imaginators Senseis show their battle class; SuperChargers vehicles show land, sea or sky.
  • Android: hold a figure against your phone to identify it with NFC (phones with a MIFARE Classic–capable NFC chip).
  • Completion by game and element, missing list, duplicates, wishlist, notes and an indicative price guide.

Questions

Is there an app to track my Skylanders collection?

Yes. PortalDex is an iPhone and Android app with a complete Skylanders checklist for all six games. You mark what you own and it shows your completion and what is missing.

Can I scan Skylanders figures with my phone?

On Android, yes: PortalDex reads Skylanders figures with NFC on phones whose NFC chip supports MIFARE Classic. Not every phone does; on those phones you add figures manually. iPhone uses manual adding.

Does PortalDex include variants like Legendary and Eon's Elite?

Yes — Legendary, Eon's Elite, LightCore, Dark, Series 2/3 reposes, chase and event variants are separate entries, so you can track each one.
