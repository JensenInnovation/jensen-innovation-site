Features

📖 Figure details

Release info, packs and sets, abilities, upgrade paths and catchphrases, tips for telling variants apart, and links to related items.

📊 Completion stats

Progress by game, set, element or series; one tap shows everything you're still missing.

💰 Price guide

Indicative, community-maintained loose and boxed values in 16 currencies.

Hold a figure against your phone to identify and add it, on phones that support it.

Frequently asked questions

What is PortalDex?

PortalDex is an iPhone, Android and Windows app for toys-to-life collectors. It tracks Skylanders figures, Skylanders cards, amiibo, Disney Infinity and LEGO Dimensions in one app.

Is PortalDex free?

Yes. PortalDex is free on the App Store, Google Play and the Microsoft Store, with no subscription.

Does PortalDex need an account or internet?

No. It works fully offline with no account, no ads and no tracking. Your collection stays on your device.

Which languages does PortalDex support?

English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional).

Does PortalDex show what my figures are worth?

It includes an indicative, community-maintained price guide with loose and boxed values in 16 currencies.

Can I export my collection?

Yes, as CSV or PDF — useful for backups, insurance and trading.

Is PortalDex official?

No. It is an independent fan app by Jensen Innovation and is not affiliated with Activision, Nintendo, Disney, the LEGO Group or Topps.

Help improve PortalDex

The catalogs, photos and prices are built together with collectors. You can contribute a figure or card photo straight from the app, and suggest price corrections on GitHub.
