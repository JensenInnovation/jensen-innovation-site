PortalDex

The collection tracker for toys-to-life collectors. Track your Skylanders figures and cards, amiibo, Disney Infinity and LEGO Dimensions — see what you own, what you're missing and what it's worth. Offline, no account.

Five collections in one app

Skylanders

All six games — figures, variants, traps, vehicles, magic items, adventure packs and creation crystals.

Skylanders Cards

Over 1,200 Topps trading cards, Battlecast cards and the cards packed with every figure.

amiibo

The full lineup across every amiibo series.

Disney Infinity

Figures, play sets, power discs and toy box games for 1.0, 2.0 and 3.0.

LEGO Dimensions

Characters, vehicles and gadgets, plus a "which pack do I need" guide.

Features

  • Figure details — release info, packs and sets, abilities, upgrade paths and catchphrases, tips for telling variants apart, and links to related items.
  • Completion stats — progress by game, set, element or series; one tap shows everything you're still missing.
  • Price guide — indicative, community-maintained loose and boxed values in 16 currencies.
  • NFC scanning (Android) — hold a figure against your phone to identify and add it, on phones that support it.
  • Collector tools — duplicates, separate decks, wishlist, personal notes, CSV and PDF export.
  • 12 languages — English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional).
  • Offline and private — no account, no ads, no tracking. Your collection stays on your device.

Help improve PortalDex

The catalogs, photos and prices are built together with collectors. You can contribute a figure or card photo straight from the app, and suggest price corrections on GitHub.
