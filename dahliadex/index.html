DahliaDex

Which dahlia is that? Point your iPhone at a bloom and DahliaDex names the most likely varieties. Then keep track of the dahlias you grow and the ones you want. Offline, no account.

App Store

iPhone: in App Store review, coming soon · Android: coming soon · 13 languages

Features

📷 Camera scan

Take or pick a photo of one bloom. DahliaDex shows the three most likely varieties with how sure it is, and reference photos to compare.

🌸 Variety catalog

Over 1,800 dahlia varieties. Filter by flower form, color and bloom size, and see which ones are most widely sold.

🌱 My Garden

A journal for the dahlias you grow: note when you plant them and in which bed, the first bloom with a photo, and when you lift and store the tubers. At the end you get a report of your season.

❄️ Tips for your season

Set where you garden and DahliaDex uses your local frost dates for planting, lifting and storage tips and reminders, in both hemispheres.

⭐ Wishlist

Keep the varieties you want for next season or tuber swaps.

🔒 Offline and private

Scanning runs on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking, and your photos stay on your device.

🌍 13 languages

English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese, Polish, Japanese, Danish, Swedish, Norwegian and Finnish.

Frequently asked questions

Can an app really tell dahlia varieties apart?

Often, but not always. Many dahlias look alike, and color changes with weather and the age of the bloom. That is why DahliaDex shows its three best guesses with reference photos, and tells you when it is not sure instead of guessing.

What does it cost?

The catalog, My Garden and Wishlist are free. You get 10 free scans; after that, unlimited scanning is a one-time in-app purchase of $9.99 (or the local equivalent). No subscription.

Does DahliaDex need internet or an account?

No. Everything, including the scan, works offline on your phone, with no account.

When is it available?

The iPhone version is in App Store review and will be on the App Store soon. The Android version is in testing and will follow on Google Play.

Help with photos

Many dahlia varieties still have no good photo. In the app, you can share a photo of a variety you grow; approved photos are credited to you and help the scan recognize more varieties. You can also email photos to dahlia@jensen-innovation.com with the variety name.
