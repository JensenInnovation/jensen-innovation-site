Privacy Policy

DahliaDex — dahlia variety identifier
Effective date: 9 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the DahliaDex app ("the app", "we") handles information. DahliaDex is developed by Jensen Innovation (Klaus Jensen), Denmark.

Summary

DahliaDex does not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal data. There are no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app makes no network connections of its own. Your photos, garden and wishlist stay on your device.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not require an account or sign-in.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, location, contacts or device identifiers through the app.
  • We do not use advertising or third-party analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • Your garden, wishlist, notes, scan history and settings are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to us or to any server.

Camera and photos

The app uses your camera, or a photo you pick from your library, only to identify the dahlia in the picture. Identification runs entirely on your device; the photo is not uploaded anywhere. The app only accesses the photos you choose.

Optional photo contributions

If you choose "Share this photo", the app removes location and other metadata from the photo and opens a prefilled email in your own mail app, addressed to dahlia@jensen-innovation.com. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. If you do:

  • We receive the photo, the variety name, the app version, the credit name you enter (if any) and your email address.
  • We use the photo to show on the variety's page in the app, credited with the name you gave, and to improve the identification model. By sending it you license the photo to us under CC BY 4.0.
  • We use your email address only to reply about your contribution. We do not add it to any mailing list or share it.
  • You can ask us to remove your photo or delete your email at any time by writing to dahlia@jensen-innovation.com; removed photos leave the app in the next update.

In-app purchase

Unlimited scanning is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. The app checks the purchase with Apple's App Store on your device; Apple's handling of that data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Links to other services

The app can open third-party websites in your browser, for example a shop that sells a variety or the source page credited for a photo. Those websites have their own privacy policies. We do not control and are not responsible for their content or practices.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to dahlia@jensen-innovation.com.
