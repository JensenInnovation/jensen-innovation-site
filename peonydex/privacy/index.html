Privacy Policy

PeonyDex — peony variety catalog and garden journal
Effective date: 28 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the PeonyDex app ("the app", "we") handles information. PeonyDex is developed by Jensen Innovation (Klaus Jensen), Denmark.

Summary

PeonyDex does not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal data. There are no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app makes no network connections of its own. Your photos, garden, notes and wishlist stay on your device.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not require an account or sign-in.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, location, contacts or device identifiers through the app.
  • We do not use advertising or third-party analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • Your garden, journal, wishlist, notes and settings are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to us or to any server. Backups are files you save yourself.
  • "Where I garden" (your country and region, used for frost dates in the guide) is chosen by you, stored on the device and never sent anywhere. The app does not use GPS.

Camera and photos

The app uses your camera, or a photo you pick from your library, only when you add a photo to your garden journal, to a variety you add yourself, or to a photo you choose to share with us. Photos stay on your device unless you send them. The app only accesses the photos you choose.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled as notifications on your device from your frost dates. Nothing is sent to us.

Optional photo contributions and feedback

If you choose "Share this photo", "Send feedback" or "Send to PeonyDex", the app removes location and other metadata from any photo and opens a prefilled email in your own mail app, addressed to peony@jensen-innovation.com. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. If you do:

  • We receive what the email contains: for a photo, the photo, the variety name, the app version, the credit name you enter (if any) and your email address.
  • We use a photo to show on the variety's page in the app, credited with the name you gave, and to train a future camera identification. By sending it you license the photo to us under CC BY 4.0.
  • We use your email address only to reply. We do not add it to any mailing list or share it.
  • You can ask us to remove your photo or delete your email at any time by writing to peony@jensen-innovation.com; removed photos leave the app in the next update.

In-app purchases

The current version of PeonyDex has no in-app purchases. If a later version offers one (for example camera identification), it will be handled by Apple, we will not receive your payment details, and this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Links to other services

The app can open third-party websites in your browser, for example a nursery that sells a variety or the source page credited for a photo. Those websites have their own privacy policies. We do not control and are not responsible for their content or practices.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to peony@jensen-innovation.com.
