PeonyDex

Which peony is that, and how do I grow it? PeonyDex is a pocket catalog of more than 2,000 peony varieties, with a garden journal for the ones you grow and a guide timed to your own frost dates. Offline, no account.

Coming soon to iPhone · 13 languages

Features

🌸 Variety catalog

More than 2,000 peonies sold by nurseries and peony farms: herbaceous, tree peonies, Itoh (intersectional) hybrids and wild species. Filter by plant group, flower form and colour.

🔎 The details that matter

Flower form from single to bomb and double, colour, bloom season and fragrance, and the other names a variety is sold under.

🌱 My Garden

A journal for the peonies you grow: where you planted them, the first shoots and the first bloom with a photo, when you divided them, and a report of your season. Peonies live for decades; your notes do too.

📖 Growing guide

18 articles on planting depth, dividing, supports, cutting for the vase, grey mould and why a peony won't flower, timed to your local frost dates, with reminders.

⭐ Wishlist

Keep the peonies you want for autumn planting.

🔒 Offline and private

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your garden and photos stay on your iPhone.

🌍 13 languages

English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese, Polish, Japanese, Danish, Swedish, Norwegian and Finnish.

Frequently asked questions

Can PeonyDex identify a peony from a photo?

Not yet. Camera identification, as in our DahliaDex app for dahlias, needs many photos of every variety, and we are collecting them now. Until then, filter the catalog by group, form, colour and season to narrow down an unlabelled peony.

What does it cost?

The catalog, My Garden, the guide and the Wishlist are free.

Does PeonyDex need internet or an account?

No. Everything works offline on your iPhone, with no account.

When is it available?

PeonyDex is being finished for iPhone. An Android version will follow, as for DahliaDex.

Help with photos

Most peony varieties still have no good photo we may show. In the app you can share a photo of a variety you grow; approved photos are credited to you and will teach the coming camera identification. You can also email photos to peony@jensen-innovation.com with the variety name.
